# Business Support Service Page

## Goal
Create a new `/business-support` page that feels like the existing Marketing and Sales pages while presenting Business Support as KGHQ’s flexible home for admin, content, systems, automation, and day-to-day execution.

## Page experience
- Add a new operations-focused hero image showing a small-business team actively managing workflows, content, and systems.
- Use the established KGHQ purple, charcoal, off-white, lime, yellow, and coral visual system with Sora and Manrope typography.
- Keep the familiar service-page rhythm while introducing original workflow graphics, task-board visuals, iconography, and operations-themed cards.
- Make the page polished and usable across desktop and mobile.

## Content and sections
1. Hero: “The extra support your business has been missing,” supporting copy, two calls to action, and three value points.
2. Outcome selector: six ways to start—work off my plate, get organized, keep content moving, repurpose content, improve operations, and automate busywork—with interactive recommendations.
3. Three support areas: Admin & Operations, Content Management, and Systems & Tech.
4. Plans and tokens: Lite, Starter, Growth, and Scale using the supplied pricing and plan details.
5. Deliverables preview: a curated mix of admin, content, and systems deliverables with token costs, plus BizPal guidance and a path to the full library.
6. Process: five steps from identifying the bottleneck to clearing the next item.
7. Why KGHQ: capacity, focus, flexibility, finishing small tasks, human-led AI support, and better systems.
8. Founder workload visual: a distinctive “somewhere to send the work” task-flow section.
9. Whole-business flexibility: Business Support, Marketing, and Sales working under one token plan.
10. FAQ based on the supplied service model and a final “Get it off your list” call to action.

## Navigation and linking
- Add “Business Support” to the homepage desktop and mobile navigation.
- Add cross-links from the Marketing and Sales service-page navigation where appropriate.
- Connect deliverable discovery to `/deliverables` and planning calls to `/new-client`.
- Add unique page title, description, Open Graph metadata, and Twitter card metadata.

## Verification
- Check the new page at desktop and phone widths.
- Test navigation, recommendation interactions, deliverables links, and calls to action.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow, runtime errors, console errors, or build errors.
