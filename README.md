Section 11: FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

BizPhone is designed to sound professional, natural, and helpful. We can also make it clear that callers are speaking with a virtual assistant if that fits your brand and compliance needs.

Can BizPhone book appointments?

Yes. Depending on your plan and tools, BizPhone can collect appointment requests, check availability, or connect with scheduling workflows.

Can it answer questions about my business?

Yes. We train your AI agent on your services, FAQs, hours, policies, locations, and preferred responses.

Can it send call summaries to my team?

Yes. BizPhone can send call summaries by email, text, CRM, spreadsheet, or workflow automation.

What happens if a call is urgent?

We can create escalation rules so urgent calls are routed or flagged immediately.

Can I use my existing phone number?

In many cases, yes. We can help set up forwarding, routing, or a dedicated BizPhone number depending on your needs.

Is this good for after-hours calls?

Yes. After-hours coverage is one of the best use cases for BizPhone.

Do you support multiple locations?

Yes. Multi-location setups are available through BizPhone Scale.

Can BizPhone replace my receptionist?

It depends on your business. BizPhone is best used to support your team, handle overflow, capture missed calls, and improve response time. For many businesses, it can reduce the need for additional front-desk coverage.

How long does setup take?

Most basic setups can be completed quickly once we have your business information, call flow, FAQs, and routing preferences.
